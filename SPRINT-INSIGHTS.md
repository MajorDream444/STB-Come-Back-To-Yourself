# Sprint Insights — Flo / Come Back To Yourself

Key principles surfaced through discovery work, client interviews, and strategic sprints.
These inform all product, content, and positioning decisions.

---

## The Flo Principle

> People do not need more information.
> People need enough safety to access the information they already carry.

The facilitator is not the answer.
The facilitator creates conditions where answers emerge.

---

## The Core Discovery (June 3 Sprint)

The previous version positioned Flo around breathwork, nervous-system regulation,
and somatic healing.

After discovery work and real client interviews, a deeper truth emerged:

**People are not responding to Flo because of a modality.**

**They are responding to the experience of safety.**

The common outcome is:

> "I finally feel safe enough to tell the truth."

The breathwork is a doorway.
The conversation is a doorway.
The bodywork is a doorway.

**The real offer is:**
A space where people can stop performing.
A space where they can stop carrying everything alone.
A space where they can finally exhale.

---

## Strategic Rules

### Solve the person in front of you before building for the crowd.

Flo has real people interested now.
The site should help those people take one simple next step.

Do not add:
- newsletters
- Substack funnels
- affiliate marketing
- automations
- complex CRM workflows

...until the manual process becomes the bottleneck.

---

### First things first.

Each version of the product should do one thing well.

V1: Help interested people take the first step.
V2: Support Flo in remembering and following up.
V3: Automate what has already become repetitive.

---

## Weekly Circle Positioning

The Weekly Breath & Integration Circle should not be branded as a "men's circle" yet.

The data is showing that men respond strongly to Flo's work.
Let the data keep showing who responds before naming the audience explicitly.

Language that works now:
> Especially supportive for people who are used to carrying everything alone.

---

## Offer Ladder (June 2026)

1. Free Discovery Call — 30 min — lowest barrier, highest trust
2. Weekly Breath & Integration Circle — 2 hrs — group container, recurring
3. Private Breathwork Journey — 90 min — signature 1:1 experience

The Nervous System Reset remains a lighter secondary option.
Do not over-feature it on the site.

---

## What Changes When Positioning Shifts

Old positioning:
> Breathwork practitioner → somatic healing → nervous-system regulation

New positioning:
> A space where people feel safe enough to tell the truth

This changes:
- How the hero reads (less modality, more felt sense)
- How the about section reads (human journey, not credential stack)
- How CTAs read (start here, not "book a session")
- How the testimonials are chosen (feelings, not outcomes)
